Dosing, care & power

Published reference facts for everyday use.

Reference

  • Dosing capsules

    Pre-fill capsules and drop one into the filling chamber instead of loading ground herb loose. They keep the chamber cleaner, make swapping loads quick, and travel well — the factory Capsule Magazine carries 8 capsules. Capsules are reusable; replace them when they get dented or hard to open.

  • Cooling unit care

    The cooling unit sits on top and holds the top screen — it's where most resin builds up. Clean it regularly: when draw starts feeling tight or flavor drops off, take the cooling unit apart and soak the parts in isopropyl alcohol, rinse with warm water, and let everything dry completely before reassembling. With daily use, a weekly soak keeps airflow open; check the screens and swap in a fresh one when brushing no longer clears it.

  • USB-C Supercharge

    The Mighty+ charges over USB-C. With a USB-C Power Delivery charger, S&B's Supercharge function brings it to roughly 80% in about 40 minutes. It can also be used while plugged in once the battery holds some charge.

  • Battery facts

    Inside are two 3,300 mAh lithium-ion cells (6,600 mAh total). The cells aren't user-replaceable — when capacity eventually fades, battery service goes through Storz & Bickel.
